# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the in-app browser. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

## Durable product decisions

- Visual direction: option 3, “Sistema de Relaciones”. The selected reference is `docs/design/reference-system-relations.png`.
- Product voice: academic, plural, traceable and sober. Separate facts, interpretations and proposals.
- Primary audience: students and researchers; public-policy practitioners are a secondary audience.
- Repository remains private until methodology, licensing and the legal core are reviewed.
- No legal instrument may be labeled both in force and verified without a dated specialist legal review.
- Every regional heat point must open its underlying case registry; national and multiregional cases require equivalent access.
- The territorial map includes a nationwide social-conflict heat layer based on the latest reviewed Defensoría del Pueblo monthly report. Always show its reporting cutoff, distinguish all social conflicts from mining conflicts, keep pilot-case markers separate, and disclose that regional reference points are not incident locations or impact areas.
- Social-conflict heat intensity must encode the selected number of cases with an explicit numeric legend: low values in cyan, medium values in amber and high values in red. Regional points use the same scale.
- The territorial map has separate toggleable layers for explicit illegal/informal-mining mentions and for aggregated vigente REINFO registrations. Both must show source and cutoff. Illegal/informal references come only from traceable records and never imply exhaustive zones or legal findings; REINFO regional points are aggregates, not activity locations, and registration never implies completed formalization.
- The territorial map has a separate “Minería formal” comparison layer. In V1 it uses MINEM plants of benefit listed as vigente as a transparent partial indicator, never concessions or project pipeline. Show source, consultation date, mapped/unmapped totals and the incomplete-universe caveat; green rings are department reference points, not plant locations or causal overlap.
- The territorial map has separate “Exploración minera” and “Concesiones mineras” layers. Exploration uses unique active-exploration expediente codes from MINEM and never implies exploitation or production. Concessions use INGEMMET rights classified as TITULADO and never imply activity, surface ownership, environmental approval or operating permission. Both show source, consultation basis, exact counting rules and department-reference caveats; use distinct concentric line styles so they remain legible with the other layers.
